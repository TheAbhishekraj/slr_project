## 2. Problem Statement
"UAVs often lack reliable heading angle measurements, especially in indoor scenarios, space, and GNSS-denied environments, posing an additional observability challenge on range-based relative localization." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"We tackle this problem by proposing a novel solution enhancing the classical tag-and-anchor trilateration." [p.1]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Relative pose estimation with approximate maximum-likelihood trilateration
Category: UWB
"We tackle this problem by proposing a novel solution enhancing the classical tag-and-anchor trilateration. The proposed solution relies on Ultra-wideband range measurements and addresses the relative pose estimation between pairs of UAVs under relative motion." [p.1]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: nano-UAVs (Crazyflie)
"a swarm of five nano-UAVs" [p.6]
- Sensors: UWB, FlowDeck v2, Loco positioning deck
"Each nano-UAV is equipped with a FlowDeck v2 that enables velocity measurements and a Loco positioning deck that allows to acquire UWB range measurements." [p.6]
- Compute: off-board computer
"The UAVs are resource-constrained, and they can not carry the computation onboard. Thus, the computer runs the relative localization and formation control algorithms previously introduced." [p.6]
- Communication: UWB
"UWB range measurements" [p.6]
