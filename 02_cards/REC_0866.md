## 2. Problem Statement
"SAR missions operate in a dangerous and unpredictable environment. It requires quick thinking, accuracy, and flexibility in reaction to changing circumstances, sometimes in dangerous settings for first responders. While somewhat successful, traditional SAR techniques are limited by human endurance, accessibility to hazardous terrain, and the capacity to act quickly when faced with inadequate information [1]."
