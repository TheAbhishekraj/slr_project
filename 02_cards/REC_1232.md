## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Range-Visual-Inertial Odometry (RVIO) with coarse-to-fine image registration
Category: HYBRID
"an efficient range-visual-inertial odometry (RVO) is proposed to provide local tracking, which utilizes measurements from a 1D Laser Range Finder (LRF) to suppress scale drift in the odometry." [p.1]
