# IEEE FORMATTING STANDARDS

## Paper
- Size: US Letter (8.5 x 11 in)
- Margins: 0.75 in top, bottom, left, right
- Columns: 2
- Column gutter: 0.25 in

## Typography
- Body font: Times New Roman, 10 pt
- Title: 24 pt, centered, Title Case
- Author: 11 pt, centered
- Abstract: 9 pt, bold italic, 150–250 words
- Keywords: 9 pt, bold italic, up to 6, alphabetical
- Body text: 10 pt, justified

## Headings
- H1 (section): I., II., III. — small caps, centered
- H2 (subsection): A., B., C. — italic, left-aligned
- H3 (subsubsection): 1), 2), 3) — italic, run-in

## Figures
- Caption: below figure, 8 pt
- Label: "Fig. 1."
- In-text: (Fig. 1)
- Single column: width = \columnwidth
- Full width: width = \textwidth

## Tables
- Caption: above table, 8 pt, small caps
- Label: "TABLE I."
- In-text: (TABLE I)
- Rules: toprule, midrule, bottomrule

## Equations
- Numbered: right-aligned, format (1)
- Referenced in text: (1)

## References
- Style: IEEEtran
- Size: 8 pt
- Indent: hanging
- Numbered [1], [2], ...
- Order: by first citation

## Citations
- Single: [1]
- Range: [1]–[3]
- Multiple: [1], [2], [4]
- Run collapse: 3+ consecutive → [1]–[3]

## Page
- Page numbers: none in body
- Headers: none
- Footers: none

## Color
- Body text: black
- Links: blue, underlined
- Figures: as produced
- No decorative color

## Language
- American spelling
- Banned words: delve, landscape (metaphor), crucial, pivotal,
  state-of-the-art (unless named baseline follows), very, really,
  It is important to note, It should be noted, In conclusion.
- Banned paragraph openers: Moreover, Furthermore, Additionally.
