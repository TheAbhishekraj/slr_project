# Slide 2: Paper Specifications
**Dimensions & Layout**
- **Size:** US Letter (**8.5 x 11 in**)
- **Margins:** **0.75 in** (top, bottom, left, right)
- **Columns:** **2**
- **Column Gutter:** **0.25 in**

---
# Slide 3: Core Typography (Part 1)
**Titles & Authors**
- **Body Font:** Times New Roman, **10 pt**
- **Title:** **24 pt**, centered, Title Case
- **Author Block:** **11 pt**, centered

---
# Slide 4: Core Typography (Part 2)
**Abstract, Keywords, & Body**
- **Abstract:** **9 pt**, **bold italic**, 150–250 words
- **Keywords:** **9 pt**, **bold italic**, up to 6 keywords (alphabetical)
- **Body Text:** **10 pt**, justified alignment

---
# Slide 5: Heading Hierarchy
**Section Structuring**
- **H1 (Section):** I., II., III. — **small caps, centered**
- **H2 (Subsection):** A., B., C. — *italic, left-aligned*
- **H3 (Subsubsection):** 1), 2), 3) — *italic, run-in*

---
# Slide 6: Figures
**Images & Diagrams**
- **Caption:** Placed **below** the figure, **8 pt** font
- **Labeling:** Formatted as "**Fig. 1.**"
- **In-text Citation:** Formatted as "(Fig. 1)"
- **Width (Single Column):** `\columnwidth`
- **Width (Full Page):** `\textwidth`

---
# Slide 7: Tables
**Data Representation**
- **Caption:** Placed **above** the table, **8 pt** font
- **Styling:** **small caps**
