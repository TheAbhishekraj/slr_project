## 1. Hardcoding vs. Dynamic Calculation in `make_figures.py`

The script was reviewed without execution to determine if it dynamically calculates the analysis figures directly from the CSV outputs or if values were manually hardcoded.

**Verification:**
- **F1 (PRISMA Flow):** The counts for F1 are **hardcoded** into the script as string tuples (e.g., `('Records identified: 2,000...', 0.95)`, `('Studies included (frozen denominator): 287', 0.17)`). This is expected as PRISMA flowchart numbers are drawn from earlier pipeline stages (database harvest and screening) rather than the final `MASTER_EVIDENCE.csv`.
- **F2 (Method Category):** **Dynamic**. Calculated via `collections.Counter()` on the `method_category_clean` column of `inference_table.csv`.
- **F3 (Primary Sensor):** **Dynamic**. Calculated from the `sensor_primary` column of `inference_table.csv`.
- **F4 (Real vs Sim):** **Dynamic**. Calculated from the `real_or_sim` column of `MASTER_EVIDENCE.csv`.
- **F5 (Year Trajectory):** **Dynamic**. Calculated from the `year` column of `MASTER_EVIDENCE.csv`.
- **F6 (Geography):** **Dynamic**. Calculated from the `country` column of `MASTER_EVIDENCE.csv` utilizing a custom normalization function (`norm_country`).
- **F7 (Quality Tier):** **Dynamic**. Calculated from the `quality_tier` column of `inference_table.csv`.
- **F8 (Fusion Method):** **Dynamic**. Calculated from the `fusion_class` column of `inference_table.csv`.
- **F9 (Numeric Accuracy Metric):** **Dynamic**. Calculated by running a regex over the concatenated `headline_result` and `metrics` columns of `MASTER_EVIDENCE.csv`.

**Finding:** **PASS.** Apart from the PRISMA flowchart (which summarizes upstream pipeline history), all data-driven figures (F2–F9) are dynamically and deterministically generated directly from the underlying CSV datasets. There is no hardcoded manipulation of the synthesis results.
